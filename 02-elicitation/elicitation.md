## Interview notes

### Current process
Students send an email to the technician to ask for equipment. The technician writes the booking in a shared spreadsheet. The student collects the equipment from the store room.
### Problems
Two people sometimes book the same item because the spreadsheet is not always updated. Nobody can easily see who has an item at the moment.
### Exceptions
If equipment is late, the technician sends an email to the student. There is no fixed rule or penalty, so it depends on the technician. Damaged items are not always reported at once.
### Information needed
The technician needs the student name, the item, the dates, and the condition of the item when it comes back. Today the condition is often not written down.
### Something we do not understand
Lecturers can take equipment outside the college, but students cannot. The technician was not sure about the exact rules for this.

## Candidate requirement
The system shall save the borrower, the equipment item, and the start and end dates for every booking.
## What Did I Learn?

### Three things I learned
• Bookings are made by email and kept in a shared spreadsheet, so double bookings can happen.

• There is no fixed rule for late returns, so each technician deals with them in a different way.

• The condition of equipment is often not recorded when it is returned.

### One thing I still do not know
• What the exact rules are for taking equipment outside the college, and who can approve this.
